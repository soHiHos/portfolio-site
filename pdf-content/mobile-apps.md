## 2. What you get

### Problems it solves

- **"We type the same data again and again."** Dispatch slips become invoices, POs go into your system, bills go into your books. All by hand.
- **"Everything happens on WhatsApp, and nothing is recorded."** Orders, production and dispatch get agreed in chats and are forgotten by evening.
- **"Our dealer or distributor network is hard to manage."** Price lists, orders and ledgers live in different places, and every dealer question turns into a phone call.
- **"Payments always come in late."** Chasing money is awkward and easy to forget, so it stays with your customers longer than it should.

### What I build

- **Procurement apps.** Supplier search, purchase requisitions and approvals from the phone, with every request recorded.
- **B2B eCommerce apps.** Your buyers browse the catalog, reorder in a few taps and check their account, ledger and order status.
- **Collaboration and companion apps.** Projects, tasks and team communication in one place, plus companion apps for the systems I build, so your team can use them on the go.
- **Mobile payment apps.** Send invoices, pay vendors and collect payments from the phone, with every payment tracked.

### Every app includes

- **Android and iPhone.** Built once, released on both.
- **Admin dashboard.** A web panel for your team to manage users, data and orders.
- **Secure login.** Each person sees only what their role allows.
- **Store publishing.** On the Play Store and App Store, under your business's name.

Typical uses: Procurement apps, B2B eCommerce apps, Collaboration & companion apps, Mobile payment apps.

### Add-on · AI, only where it's worth it

Design this as a highlighted callout (amber border), like the AI add-on on the website.

Not every app needs AI. Most screens work best as plain, fast forms and lists. During discovery I'll suggest AI only where it makes the app easier to use and is worth its running cost for how much it'll be used. Quoted separately; AI usage is billed at cost.

- **Snap and fill.** Take a photo of a bill, label or order slip, and the app fills in the form.
- **Smart search.** Buyers and staff find products, suppliers or past orders by describing what they need.
- **In-app assistant.** Answers common questions from your catalog, policies and order status, any time of day.

*AI can make mistakes, including wrong or made-up answers, so I build it to suggest and fill in, and people check before it counts. I'm not responsible for its output or for decisions based on it.*

### Included in the monthly plan

- Hosting, backups and uptime monitoring.
- Bug fixes: anything that does not work the way the signed scope says. Critical issues (the system is down or blocks work) get a reply within 1 business day, everything else within 3 business days (Monday to Friday).
- Security and dependency updates.
- For the first 90 days after go-live: adjustments to what I built (not new features).
- After 90 days: small changes up to 3 hours a month. Unused hours do not roll over.

### Not included, quoted or billed at cost

- New screens, reports, fields or modules.
- Changing how something works when it already works as agreed.
- New integrations (WhatsApp, payments, Tally and similar) and new third-party services.
- Anything beyond the 3 hours in a month. Bigger changes are quoted first, at a day rate of ₹10,000.

**The simple test:** if it doesn't work the way we agreed, I fix it free. If it works as agreed and you want it different, that's a change.

### Third-party and usage costs (never in the monthly plan)

- **Play Store and App Store developer accounts** are created in your business's name. You pay their fees directly to Google and Apple: a one-time fee of about ₹2,400 for Google Play and about ₹9,000 a year for Apple.
- **Push notifications, SMS, email, maps and payment gateway fees**, if the app uses them: billed at cost as separate lines, named in the scope.
- **OTP login**, if you opt for it: the DLT registration needed to send SMS in India and the SMS or WhatsApp message charges are paid by you.
- **Domain and backend hosting** are part of the monthly plan's hosting; third-party usage fees are not. If you buy the code outright, the server and backend hosting are yours to run and pay for, or add a maintenance plan.

Every third-party service the build uses is named in the signed scope before work starts, with what it is used for and that it is billed at cost. A new one added later counts as a change and is quoted.

## 4. Monthly plan or own the code

Two ways to pay for the same software. **The monthly plan** costs less up front and I host, maintain and support it. **Owning the code** means you receive the code as it stands on the day you buy and the monthly plan ends; hosting is then yours to run, or you can add a maintenance plan.

### The rules

- The price of owning the code depends on your project and is agreed before you commit.
- If you start on the monthly plan and buy later, the **setup you've paid is deducted** and **50% of the monthly fees you've paid also count toward it**.
- The price never goes below **25% of the agreed code price**, so staying longer never makes the code free.
- You can buy at any time. After buying: a 30-day free bug-fix window after handover; hosting is yours to run, or add a maintenance plan (3-month minimum) that covers bug fixes, security updates and up to 3 hours of small changes a month. Problems caused by your own edits to the code are quoted separately.
- The reusable core of my platform stays licensed for your own business only. Your data is always yours.

## 6. How a project runs

1. **Fill out the form, then a free call or Google Meet.** Tell me which services you need. I reply by email or WhatsApp within 48 hours, and we set up a call where you walk me through how things work today, your budget and when you'd like to start. I'll tell you whether you need custom software at all, or whether a free product already does the same job.
2. **Discovery, then a written proposal in 5–7 days.** If you're in or around Raipur, I visit your office, shop or warehouse to see how work gets done and which software, sheets and apps you use. If you're further away, you walk me through it on a screen share. You work with me personally throughout, from this first look to launch, with no salesperson or middleman in between. Within 5–7 days of the visit or walkthrough you get a written proposal, free and with no obligation: how I understand your business, what the system will do, and the scope, price, timeline and payment schedule. It stays valid for 21 days, and the detailed design comes after you approve it. If you'd like a deeper analysis before deciding, the paid workflow audit gives you a written report you keep, whether or not we build.
3. **Prototype, then built in stages.** Once you approve the proposal and pay 50% of the setup fee, I work out the detailed design and build a prototype as the first milestone. Your team signs it off before the full build. For larger systems we start with one workflow and expand. You see a demo at each milestone, and changes within the agreed scope are included; new features are quoted separately. The other 50% is due when it goes to live testing.
4. **Launch and training.** I set it up and run a handover session so your team knows how to use it. If you need more, such as extra sessions or on-site training, it's quoted in your proposal.
5. **After go-live.** The monthly plan keeps it hosted, up and secure, with bug fixes. We review how it's being used at 60–90 days. After the first 90 days, small changes up to 3 hours a month are included; bigger changes are quoted. If you buy the code outright, you get 30 days of free bug fixes after handover; after that, hosting is yours to run, or a maintenance plan covers bug fixes, security updates and up to 3 hours of small changes a month.

**Typical timeline:** 4–12 weeks for the full build, counted from the day you've sent your data and approvals. Your proposal says exactly. I'll sign an NDA before we talk if you want one.

### What I need from you

- The screens and roles as you imagine them; I turn them into a prototype for sign-off before the full build.
- A Google Play Console and Apple Developer account in your business's name (I can guide you, or you add me as a developer).
- Your brand assets (logo, colours), if you have them.
- One person who approves each stage.

## 7. Questions clients ask

**Do you build for both Android and iPhone?**
Yes. Each app is built once in Flutter, Google's toolkit for making one app that runs on both, and released on both, so you don't pay for two separate apps.

**Do I need both Android and iPhone?**
Not always. An app for your own staff can go Android only if everyone on the team uses Android phones, which also saves the yearly Apple fee. Because the app is built once for both, adding iPhone later means testing and publishing it, not rebuilding it.

**Who owns and pays for the app store accounts?**
Your business. The Play Store and App Store accounts are created in your name, and you pay their fees directly to Google and Apple: a one-time fee of about ₹2,400 for Google Play and about ₹9,000 a year for Apple. I guide you through setting them up, then manage them for you, or you add me as a developer.

**How long do the app stores take to approve the app?**
Typically 7–14 days after I submit it, and sometimes longer. Apple and Google review every new app and every update, and sometimes ask for changes before approving; I handle the submission and anything the reviewers ask for. The store review is built into the timeline in your proposal.

**How do updates reach the people using the app?**
Through the Play Store and App Store, like any other app update. Bug fixes are covered by the monthly plan, and after the first 90 days small changes up to 3 hours a month are included; new screens or features are quoted first.

**Can the app connect to software we already use?**
Yes. The app can work with systems you already have, such as Tally, your website or a payment gateway. Each connection is named and priced in the scope before work starts.

**Does the app work on weak internet?**
Apps used on site or on the road can be built to keep working on weak or no internet and sync once the connection is back. This is agreed in the scope.

**Our team isn't very tech-savvy. Will this work for us?**  
Most likely. I keep the screens simple so teams pick them up quickly, and they work on a phone. A handover session is included in setup; extra or on-site training is quoted in your proposal. You'll need one person on your side who owns it day to day.

**Whose name are the accounts in?**  
Yours. App store accounts, your domain, your WhatsApp number and payment gateway are all created in your business's name. For the domain, you can buy it yourself and give me access, or I can buy it for you; either way it sits in your own account. I manage the rest for you, or you add me as a developer.

**Can you build an app for millions of users?**  
That's not what I focus on. I build systems for businesses and their teams, dealers and customers, from a handful of users up to typically 50,000 to 90,000 monthly active users. I can also build MVPs designed to grow to that size. I'm a one-person studio, and that focus keeps your costs predictable and lets me give each client proper attention. If you're building a mass-market consumer app, a larger team will serve you better, and I'm happy to point you to one.

**Can I see a demo built for my business before committing?**  
Yes. I can build a working demo around your own process and data, so you see exactly how it would work for you. It's priced as a small share of the estimated project fee, confirmed once we've discussed the scope of work (SOW).

**How does the monthly plan work?**  
You pay a setup fee to get the software built, then a monthly fee to use it. Think of it as a licence: you use the software for as long as the plan runs, and you can buy the code whenever you like. The plan covers hosting, backups, uptime monitoring, bug fixes and security updates. For the first 90 days after go-live, adjustments to what I built are included. After that, up to 3 hours of small changes a month are included. Bigger changes are quoted before I start.

**Does the monthly fee go up for a bigger project?**  
A bigger system has more to host and support, so the monthly plan rises with the size of the project, but more slowly than the setup fee. As a rule of thumb, if the setup fee doubles, the monthly plan goes up by about 50%. The exact figures are agreed in your quote.

**What counts as a change?**  
If something doesn't work the way we agreed, it's a bug and I fix it free. If it works as agreed and you'd like it to work differently, or you want something new, that's a change.

**How quickly do you fix problems?**  
On working days (Monday to Friday), I reply to anything that stops your work within 1 business day, and to everything else within 3 business days.

**What if we want to own the code?**  
You can, at any time. You receive the code as it stands on the day you buy, and the monthly plan ends. The price is agreed before you commit, and what you've already paid counts toward it: the setup fee, plus 50% of your monthly fees. The price never drops below 25% of the agreed code price. For example, a client with a ₹60,000 setup, a ₹15,000 monthly plan and a ₹1,25,000 code price who decides to buy the code after 4 months pays ₹1,25,000 − ₹60,000 − ₹30,000 = ₹35,000. After buying, you can run hosting yourself or keep me on a maintenance plan.

**Who owns our data?**  
You do, always. If the plan ends, you get a full export of your data. See also "What if we want to own the code?"

**Are there other costs?**  
GST at 18% is added to all prices. AI usage and third-party services such as WhatsApp are billed at cost, as separate lines on your monthly bill. Every service your system uses is listed in the agreement before work starts, so nothing new appears later without your approval.

**What if we want to stop?**  
Monthly plans start with a 6-month minimum. If you need to leave inside those 6 months, you can choose: pay two months' plan fees and end the plan, or buy the code. After 6 months you can leave with 30 days' notice. Either way you get an export of your data, and the accounts stay in your name.

**What if you stop working on this?**  
If I ever stop offering the service, the code and your data pass to you free of charge, so your business is never stuck.

**How do payments work?**  
Setup is paid in two parts: 50% at signing and 50% when it goes to live testing. Live testing is when real users first start using it, before it goes fully live. The monthly plan starts at go-live, after live testing, and is invoiced each month. UPI is accepted.
