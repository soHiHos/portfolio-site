## 5. How a project runs

1. **Fill out the form, then a free call or Google Meet.** I reply within 48 hours by email or WhatsApp, and on a free call we go through what the site needs to do, your budget and when you'd like it live.
2. **A written proposal in 2–3 days.** Within 2–3 days you get the package, price and timeline in writing, free and with no obligation, valid for 21 days. Approve it and pay 50% to start.
3. **Build, with a live preview.** I start building straight away and share a live preview for you to review. Changes within the agreed package are included.
4. **Final payment, then launch.** Pay the remaining 50% before launch; then the site goes live and the files are yours, with the domain in your own account.
5. **After launch, an optional care plan.** An optional care plan with no minimum covers small edits after launch; the higher plan adds analytics and traffic reports.

**Typical timeline:** 1–5 weeks, counted from the day you've sent your data and approvals. Your proposal says exactly. I'll sign an NDA before we talk if you want one.

- Your text and photos for each page. If you don't have them yet, tell me on the call and we'll agree in the proposal how to handle them.
- Your brand assets (logo, colours), if you have them.
- Access to your domain, or a go-ahead for me to buy it in your business's name.
- One person who approves the preview and the launch.

## 6. Questions clients ask

**Will the website work well on phones?**
Yes. Every site is designed for phones first, since that's where most of your visitors are, then checked on tablets and desktops.

**Can I update the content myself?**
On the Business and Signature packages, yes: you get a simple editor (a CMS) for text, images and pages. On Launch, small edits are covered by the optional care plan.

**Will people find my site on Google?**
Every site gets the SEO basics: fast loading, clear page titles and descriptions, and a sitemap so Google can find every page. Business and Signature also get basic GEO (generative engine optimisation): structured data and clearly written pages, so AI answer tools such as ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews can understand your business and mention it. Signature adds advanced SEO and speed tuning. Rankings and mentions depend on your market, so I don't promise positions.

**Do I own the website?**
Yes. The site files are yours after final payment. For the domain, you can buy it yourself and give me access, and I'll guide you if you're not sure how, or I can buy it for you in your business's name at cost. Either way it ends up in your own account, so you always control it.

**Do I need a care plan?**
No. Both care plans are optional and have no minimum. Base care keeps the site hosted, backed up and monitored, with one small edit a month. Plus care adds visitor analytics and traffic reports. If you cancel mid-month, that month is still billed in full.

**How long does it take?**
Typically 1–5 weeks, counted from the day you've sent your text, photos and approvals. Your proposal says exactly.

**Can visitors message me on WhatsApp from the site?**
Yes. Every package sends enquiries to your WhatsApp or inbox. Launch has a contact form and a WhatsApp button; Business captures leads into WhatsApp or your CRM; Signature adds a lead dashboard so you can see which enquiries have been followed up.

**Are there other costs?**
GST at 18% is added to all prices. The domain is at cost, and payment gateway fees, AI usage and any other third-party service are billed at cost. Every service your site uses is listed in the proposal before work starts, so nothing new appears later without your approval.

**How do payments work?**
The build is paid in two parts: 50% to start and 50% before launch. The care plan, if you choose one, is invoiced each month. UPI is accepted.
