Find the offer

Header SERVICES link, home Charter strip (4 tiles), or an Ask-tower service card.

GATE 2 · /services

Pick a flight

"Find your flight" quiz, then the Websites / Custom builds / Lease tabs. Deep links like #erp open the right tab.

Pre-filled form

/?brief=<id>#contact or the assistant's tower:brief event pre-selects the service.

GATE 4 · TRANSMIT

Mail app opens

The brief goes out as a mailto:. No backend, so there's no record of briefs that were started but never sent.

Before changing anything

Guardrails from the repo rules

Content first

Most items above only touch services.json or projects.json. The assistant prompt, enums, finder and brief dropdown all read from those files, so they stay in sync without extra wiring.

No new assistant routes or templates

Website classes and lease go into the existing service and brief slots through /api/ask. Adding enum values doesn't change model routing; gpt-4o-mini stays the default.

Fallback must still render

Any change to the tower or schema needs a check that /services and the home Charter strip still render with the ask bar broken or offline.

Built from a read of services.json, services/page.tsx, services-explorer.tsx, services-section.tsx, contact-section.tsx, api/ask/content.ts and api/ask/schema.ts. Nothing in the repo was changed.
